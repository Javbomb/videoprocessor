Waveform

Open a photo or video of your projector screen, or use the live camera.

  1. Project a 100% white frame and lock the camera's exposure so the white isn't clipped.
  2. Open that shot and use Calibrate → Set white on the screen.
  3. Open shots of your content taken at the same exposure. The waveform now reads 100% / 1023 at projector peak white.
